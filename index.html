Sauco

Launch an online store in minutes, for any shop, anywhere

Rails 8 Hotwire PostgreSQL Telegram Bot Mercado Pago AI agents

A multi-tenant SaaS that lets any small business spin up its own online store in minutes, on its own subdomain or custom domain. It started with local shops in Tandil, but it's built for any city. Owners manage their catalog from where they already are: they send product photos to a Telegram bot and it turns them into listings.

  • Catalog upload through a Telegram bot, with AI-drafted products the owner reviews before publishing
  • Agentic landing pages generated by an AI agent for each store
  • Multi-tenant storefronts with themes, custom domains and Mercado Pago checkout
  • Full admin dashboard for orders, products, analytics and store settings
visit sauco.app

Hilo

Patient-centered digital health record platform

Rails 8 PostgreSQL OpenAI API Capacitor PWA

Multi-role platform (patient, doctor, clinic) with granular, permission-based access control per patient. The patient owns their medical information, can share it securely and temporarily, and use AI to understand documents that used to require a doctor to interpret.

  • AI-powered analysis of medical documents, with user confirmation before persisting
  • Dynamic PDF generation of medical summaries
  • Secure sharing via PIN-protected temporary links with access auditing
  • iOS and Android app with Capacitor, reusing the same Rails app
